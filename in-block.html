A paragraph is a self-contained unit of writing that develops a single, central idea. Structurally, it typically begins with a topic sentence that introduces the main focus, followed by supporting sentences that provide evidence, details, or examples, and concludes with a transition or summary sentence. While academic and formal writing often favors a length of three to five sentences to fully expand on a thought, a paragraph can realistically range from a single word to an entire page depending on the medium, audience, and narrative pace. Ultimately, the primary rule of paragraphing is unity and coherence, ensuring that every sentence naturally flows into the next without introducing off-topic distractions."
